Deep Tech Intelligence Stack

Five components, one join key. This is the visual companion to the textual briefing note — for driving the specification of the two proposed components.

The five components

  • BuiltQUILT
    Innovate UK grant data, classified by a Deep Tech Ontology.
  • BuiltFunding stack analyser (dtfunding)
    Capital structure reconstructed from Companies House filings.
  • BuiltFounderfluence
    Founder Mode framework tested against interviews and press.
  • ProposedPolicy tracker
    Six-category taxonomy applied to strategy and policy documents.
  • ProposedInvestor tracker
    Investor entity graph scoped to frontier-tech company stakes.

The stack, and how it converges

Every column resolves to the same company via its Companies House number — that's what makes the fifth tier possible.

Six linkages, in order of how they'd get built

  1. 01
    QUILT → Investor tracker
    Frontier-tech tags define the investor tracker's scope — not all UK investors, only those in QUILT-tagged companies.
  2. 02
    QUILT ↔ Policy tracker
    Funding deployment checked against policy signal, by technology category — the two-clock mismatch, made measurable.
  3. 03
    dtfunding → Investor tracker
    Reuses the existing SH01/CS01/AR01 extraction; adds the PSC register to unwind fund vehicles to the people behind them.
  4. 04
    dtfunding → Founderfluence
    Dated capital events replace manually-tracked milestones — the funding lens, turning role-mix into a real time series.
  5. 05
    Policy tracker ↔ Investor tracker
    Named policy leaders cross-reference against PSC/GP identities — surfacing people who sit on both sides.
  6. 06
    Government-backed cascade
    BBB/NSSIF public LP commitments traced through Companies House, cross-referenced against QUILT's grant list — two government channels, separated.

Investor tracker — entity resolution pipeline

Entity resolution — collapsing name variants into one canonical investor ID — is the actual build. Everything else reuses extraction that already exists.

What it's for

Advisory outputs

Due diligence and policy guidance — the near-term application.

Book empirical spine

Crowd-in/crowd-out test and two-clock mismatch validation — the dataset the causal-inference chapter needs.

Carry into specification

  • LP disclosure timing. UK limited partnership transparency reforms are phasing in through 2026. Historical filings stay thin; anything filed forward should improve.
  • Offshore blind spot. Non-UK LP vehicles won't appear in Companies House. The government-LP cascade is largely insulated from this; a complete private-LP picture is not.
